The Fragrance

Le Male Le Parfum

Jean Paul Gaultier • Parfum • 125ml

A bold evolution of the legendary Le Male. This powerful parfum concentration enhances the iconic DNA with deeper warmth, smooth spices, and creamy vanilla, creating a commanding scent for the modern man.

Dressed in its striking black-and-gold officer's uniform bottle, it embodies authority, charisma, and refined masculinity.

The Brand

About Quadra

Quadra comes from the Latin word for four, and it represents the four members behind this project. Each of us brings a different idea, and together we created one brand with a single clear identity.

This commercial presents Jean Paul Gaultier Le Male Le Parfum, a fragrance that blends authority, elegance, and charm in every spray.

Simple in design and bold in character, Quadra shows that true elegance does not need to be loud.
